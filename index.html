Strategy
IONS · research platform

IONS turns labs and scientists into an on-demand R&D bench

  • Labs and scientists list what they can do; IONS matches them to R&D requests.
  • Each request stays open 7 days. Labs reply with proposals and run the project in a shared workspace.
  • Built-in tools: Deep Research, Market Pulse, Demand Catalyst.
  • Main gap: no verified track record per lab, and IP terms are not public.

What's working

The front end is further along than it looks

Real, structured demand

RFQs carry CAS, purity, volume, required equipment and certifications. Example: PROJ-1102, HFPO, 100 MT/yr, NLT 98%, pressure-rated reactors. Directories list products; Scinode lists projects.

A three-sided loop

Buyer RFQ → matched plants (ATOMS) and labs (IONS) → proposals → Scimplify executes. The inside-sales workflow, partly productised.

Monetisation already being tested

Priority Partner at ₹5 lakh/yr: unlimited proposals, exclusive projects, intel reports, demand generation for 5 products.

The right schema, waiting for data

Reaction types, reactor capacity in KL, materials of construction, EHS and utilities: exactly what unit-operation matching needs.

Findings
What's missing

The gap: the data underneath isn't reliable yet

01

The numbers don't match

Different pages give different counts for the same thing. Research partners show as 1,000+, 600+, 400+ and 200+. Some counters show "0+".

02

Categories are messy

The same industry appears under two names, like "Dyes & Pigments" and "Dyes and Pigments". Anyone can add a new one, so the list keeps growing.

03

Buyer requests are incomplete

Many have no chemical ID number or country. About 60 share the same placeholder quantity. Some sit in the wrong industry.

04

Plants describe themselves, and no one checks

A plant can tick "we do nitration" with no proof it has done it, at what size, or how well.

05

Plants have no reason to log in every day

The portal only offers sales leads. It doesn't help run the factory.

06

Data safety is a promise, not a policy

The app says data is secure, but there are no published rules on who can see a plant's data. Four brand names (Scinode, IONS, ATOMS, Scira) also confuse users.

Challenges

Eight risks, and how to defuse each

01

Conflict of interest

Plants fear a competitor learns their process.

Data charter, firewall from trading desks, aggregate-only views.

02

Cold start on data

Intelligence is weak until enough verified records exist.

Start with Scimplify's own orders: no permission needed.

03

Disintermediation

Buyers and plants meet on the platform, then go direct.

Keep QA, documents, credit and logistics inside Scimplify.

04

Low digital maturity in SME plants

Paper BPRs, WhatsApp, no IT staff.

Phone-first, Hindi/Gujarati UI, photo-to-record capture.

05

AI accuracy and safety

Wrong routes or hazards in chemistry are costly.

Expert-graded evals, human approval, cite the source batch.

06

Verification cost

Audits for 500 plants are slow and expensive.

Verify through executed orders first; audit only the top plants.

07

Compliance

India's DPDP Act, export controls on critical materials, buyer IP.

Legal review of the data charter; flag controlled substances.

08

Focus and capacity

The initiative has been deprioritised before; the team is ~3 people.

Monthly milestones tied to core revenue metrics.
